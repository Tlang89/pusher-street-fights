# THE PU$HER: STREET FIGHTS
## MASTER DEVELOPMENT PLAN — VERSION 1.0

STATUS: LOCKED DEVELOPMENT BASELINE

1. ORIGINAL VISION
1990s arcade fighting game inspired by Mortal Kombat, Killer Instinct and Street Fighter, featuring original 1950s comic-book artwork and gritty city environments.

2. CHARACTER ARTWORK
Never alter or replace original approved character images, faces, clothing, bodies, or identities. Use actual approved assets, not generated substitutes.

3. APPROVED OPENING
Preserve the original 11.8-second Chicago cinematic, approved music, bass, hi-hats, street ambience, and car-door sound. No rain audio. Do not recreate or replace approved media.

4. GAME SCREENS
Chicago opening, title menu, character selection, city/stage selection, fighting arena, victory/defeat screen, and between-fight character cards.

5. CONTROLS
Landscape iPhone layout with directional pad, X/Y/A/B, Start/Select, and L/R controls. No scrolling. Buttons must function.

6. FIGHTING
Walking, jumping, crouching, blocking, punches, kicks, combos, special attacks, health bars, knockdowns, and computer opponents.

7. AUDIO
Use approved music and synchronized movement, jeans rustling, footsteps, punches, kicks, and impact effects. Verify actual playback.

8. STORY
Travel through cities, build reputation, pursue the Million Dollar Mixtape, and earn the title of The Pu$her.

9. DEVELOPMENT
Develop in the separate GitHub repository Tlang89/pusher-street-fights. Do not change the original Market Run repository. Safari publishing is optional.

10. TESTING RULES
Never redesign approved artwork or scenes during testing. Fix technical failures without changing the design. Keep previous approved versions. Do not claim a test works without verification.

11. DEVELOPMENT ORDER
Approved assets, fighting-screen design, animations, audio, playable round, character cards, city progression, full game.
